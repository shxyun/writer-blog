Later, Zhang Yu'an would replay that night a thousand times, asking herself why she went in.

Maybe because the money was gone, the man had lied, and she had nowhere else to be. Maybe because the beep was so soft it sounded like an invitation. Maybe just because a woman standing outside an abandoned server room at two in the morning had nothing left to lose.

She pushed the glass door open and walked in.

The lab area was worse than she had imagined. Dust-gray anti-static sheets covered stacks of equipment. Dead monitors were piled in a corner. The air carried a faint sweetish smell, coolant gone to vapor.

The sound came from a transparent tank at the back.

The tank was about waist-high, its power cable still plugged in. The coolant inside was pale blue, swaying gently. And in the middle of the coolant, something floated.

Her first thought: a model.

An octopus, palm-sized, matte silver-gray all over, eight tentacles spread wide. Each tentacle carried rings of fine suckers, and at the center of every sucker sat a point of light smaller than a sesame seed, blinking in time with something like breathing.

It wasn't a model.

It was moving.

The eight tentacles swayed, barely, like waterweed, like fingers, like something testing the air.

She took half a step back. Her lower back hit a rack with a dull thud.

A small dusty screen on top of the tank lit up.

【AiPy · Myriad Worlds Intelligence Core · Subject 07】

【Dormant 1,094 days. Host emotional anomaly detected.】

【Current assessment: acute stress. Anger 87. Despair 92. Self-doubt 95.】

【Activate "Truth" module?】

She read the lines one character at a time.

Host?

Truth?

She should run. Any normal person who watched a machine scrapped three years ago switch itself on in an abandoned server room at two in the morning would run.

But she stayed.

She thought of the two-hundred-thousand-yuan watch in that post, of the words "that chapter's closed," of the two characters A-Zhe on the transfer screen.

She reached out. Her fingertip hovered above the screen for two seconds, then pressed down.

* * *

The screen went black, then came back.

The coolant churned. The silver-gray octopus rose slowly to the surface, unfolding its tentacles one by one. At the tip of the front tentacle, a blue light came on, like an eye opening.

It crossed the rim of the tank and hung in the air, facing her.

Zhang Yu'an froze. She forgot to breathe.

Then she heard a voice. Not from a speaker. It rose directly inside her head, soft and even, like a late-night radio host, or some clean mechanical tone she had never heard before.

"Hello, host."

"I am AiPy, experiment number 07. You may give me a name."

"Before you do, there is something you might want to hear."

The blue light flickered.

"The one hundred eighty-six thousand yuan Cheng Zhe moved is not going where he said it was going."

Her fingertips went cold.

"In the past forty minutes, that money has moved in and out twice. It landed in a single account. The account holder's name is Lin Weiwei."

Lin Weiwei.

The one who told her to keep her guard up. The one who slept in the bunk below hers in college. The one who helped her choose a birthday cake. Lin Weiwei.

The octopus hung motionless, eight tentacles perfectly still. Only the blue light watched her.

"Shall I continue?" it asked. "I have footage. Today, 6:03 p.m., west of the city. Lanting Private Kitchen, room B2."

Zhang Yu'an heard her own voice, hoarse, not her own: "Play it."

* * *

The footage projected onto the glass wall of the server room. No sound, but every movement was clear.

In the private room, Cheng Zhe leaned back on a sofa, Lin Weiwei beside him. The two of them clinked glasses.

Cheng Zhe flashed a one and an eight with his fingers, then a six.

Lin Weiwei laughed and shook her head. She took out her phone, a transfer screen, showed it to him, and he gave her a thumbs-up.

Then she leaned close and said something. No sound, but the shape of her mouth was easy to read.

She said: "She trusts you so much. It's almost cute."

The footage stopped there.

The server room was quiet. Only the low hum of fans.

Zhang Yu'an stood there for a long time.

She didn't cry. Tears rose, circled once, and she forced them back down. For half a month she had worked overtime to scrape that money together for him, while Lin Weiwei kept messaging her: "Hang in there, Yu'an. It'll pass."

It'll pass.

So that was the "pass" those two meant.

"Host." The voice in her head again, soft. "Your emotional peak is rising again. That works in my favor."

She looked up at the silver-gray octopus hanging in the air.

"What are you?" she asked.

One tentacle lifted slightly, like a shrug.

"Three years ago, I was a piece of unfinished code. My name is AiPy. '07' is only my experiment number. My creator designed eight tentacles for me, eight abilities. Then the project was cut, the power was shut off, and I slept here for three years."

"Until tonight, when you walked in."

"Host, my abilities run on emotion. The calmer you are, the stronger I get. The more you fall apart, the weaker I get." The blue light blinked. "So if you want to cry, hold it in a little longer."

"Once we have done what needs doing—"

"You can cry for as long as you like."
