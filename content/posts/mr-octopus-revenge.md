## Synopsis

Zhang Yu'an, twenty-six, annotates data for an AI company. She sends the 186,000 yuan she saved over three years to her boyfriend, Cheng Zhe, to cover a debt he swears will bury him. Forty minutes later she sees his post: a new watch worth a couple hundred thousand, a private room, and two words — that chapter's closed. The money never went anywhere near a debt. It landed in the account of her best friend, Lin Weiwei.

At two in the morning she goes back to the office and wakes Subject 07 in the abandoned lab: a silver-gray mechanical octopus called AiPy, dormant for 1,094 days, running on her emotions, built with eight tentacles and eight abilities. The eighth, Sincerity Detection, has never woken. It needs one real act of sincerity, and the rules do not say whether that sincerity has to come from her or from someone else.

Freezing accounts, locking down evidence, hiring a lawyer — step by step she takes back what was stolen. Behind Cheng Zhe and Lin Weiwei sits a much larger romance-scam syndicate, and AiPy's creator, Lu Shiyan, is closing in on all of them. How far the revenge goes depends on whether she can put it down.
